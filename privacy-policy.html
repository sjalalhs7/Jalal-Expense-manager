Privacy Policy

Jalal Expense Manager
Effective date: 24 November 2026

Summary

Jalal Expense Manager is an offline-first expense tracking app. The app does not send your expense records, income records, budget, or app settings to the developer or to a server.

Information stored on your device

Expenses, income, budget settings, language preference, and the optional app PIN are stored locally in the app's WebView storage on your device. The optional PIN is a convenience lock only; it is not encryption and should not be treated as strong protection for financial information.

Backups

You can choose to export a JSON backup or import a backup file. These actions are initiated by you through Android's document picker. The app does not automatically upload backups. You are responsible for choosing a safe backup location and protecting exported files.

Collection, sharing, analytics and advertising

The app does not include an account system, cloud synchronization, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or network permission. The developer does not collect or share information from the app.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain in local app storage until you delete them using the app's Clear data feature or clear the app's storage/uninstall the app through Android settings. Export a backup before deleting app data. The developer cannot recover locally stored records.

Children

The app is a general-purpose expense tracker and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. For privacy questions, use the developer contact email displayed on the app's Google Play Store listing.
