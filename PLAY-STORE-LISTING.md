# Google Play Store listing draft

**App name:** Jalal Expense Manager

**Short description (under 80 characters):** Track income, expenses, budgets and reports privately on your device.

**Full description:**

Jalal Expense Manager helps you keep a clear record of everyday spending and income.

FEATURES
- Record expenses and income.
- Organize spending by category.
- Set a monthly budget.
- Review daily, weekly, monthly and yearly reports.
- Search and filter expense records.
- Export and restore your records using JSON backup files.
- Optional app PIN convenience lock.

PRIVACY
Your records are stored locally on your device. The app does not provide cloud sync or an online account. Back up your records regularly. The optional PIN is a convenience lock and does not encrypt your data.

**Suggested category:** Finance

**Ads:** No ads are currently included in the app.

**Data Safety draft (verify against the final merged manifest and every included SDK):** No data is transmitted to the developer or third parties; no analytics or advertising SDKs are included.
